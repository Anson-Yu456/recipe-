How to make Pizza

Description

This recipe is designed to be an easy, beginner-friendly homemade pizza dough. It uses simple pantry ingredients, requires only one mixing bowl, very little kneading, and rises in about 30 minutes. The dough can be made with either all-purpose flour for a softer crust or bread flour for a crispier crust.

Ingredients

  • 2 to 2⅓ cups all-purpose flour or bread flour
  • 1 packet (2¼ teaspoons) instant yeast
  • 1½ teaspoons sugar
  • ¾ teaspoon salt
  • ⅛–¼ teaspoon garlic powder or dried basil (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • ¾ cup warm water (about 105–115°F / 40–46°C)
  • Toppings of choice

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, combine 1 cup of the flour, yeast, sugar, salt, and any optional seasonings.
  2. Stir in the warm water and olive oil.
  3. Gradually mix in the remaining flour until a soft, slightly sticky dough forms.
  4. Place the dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover it, and let it rise in a warm place for about 30 minutes or until doubled in size.
  5. Gently press down the dough and shape it into a 10–12 inch pizza crust.
  6. Place the dough on a pizza pan, brush it lightly with olive oil, and poke small holes in the center with a fork to prevent large bubbles.
  7. Add your favorite sauce, cheese, and toppings.
  8. Bake in a preheated 425°F (215°C) oven for 13–15 minutes, or until the crust is golden and the toppings are cooked.
